# Authorship and public content

All work in this repository is authored by the ICARUS team.

## 1. Never credit or describe how the work was produced

Nothing we produce may credit, name, link to, or describe the tools, editors, extensions, services, or automation used to produce it. Describe **what** changed and **why**, never **how it was produced**:

- No `Co-Authored-By` trailers except for real team members.
- No `Generated with`, `Created by`, or `assisted` lines, and no session or conversation links.
- No names of AI models, agents, or development tooling in commit messages, pull requests, issues, comments, UI copy, code, or file paths.
- No hard-coded paths into personal tool folders, editor data directories, or user profiles.

Write in the voice of the team ("we", "the team").

## 2. Never use em dashes

Covered in full by the `no-em-dashes` rule. Never use U+2014, U+2013, U+2015, or ` -- ` as a dash in any new or modified file, commit, PR, issue, or chat response.
